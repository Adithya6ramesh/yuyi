[01] THE PROBLEM

Wind is the cheapest power on the grid. It is also the hardest to plan for.

a.
It changes its mind.
Output swings by thousands of megawatts in a day. Orders go in the day before.
b.
Forecasts keep rewriting themselves.
One half-hour gets many forecasts. Only those published before your deadline count.
c.
A single number hides the risk.
Without a range, nobody knows how much margin to buy.

[02] THE METHOD

Three moves from raw data to a confident order.

{{pipeline}}
STEP 01
Collect the official data
Actual wind output and every published forecast, downloaded once, cleaned and checked for gaps.
GET /datasets/FUELHH/stream · fuelType=WIND
STEP 02
Pick the forecast you'd really have had
For each half-hour, the latest forecast published before your deadline. Anything later is ignored.
forecast(T) = latest where publishTime ≤ T − H
STEP 03
Measure, then plan a cushion
Every miss is recorded. The bad days become a safety margin, tested on days it never saw.
miss = actual − forecast · cushion = p05(miss)
